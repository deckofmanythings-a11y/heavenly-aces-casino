How to play Huff n' Puff

243 Ways. Match symbols on adjacent reels starting from the left — position on the reel doesn't matter. The Wolf is Wild and substitutes for all pay symbols.

💰 Moneybags. Land 6 or more moneybags anywhere to start the House feature.

🛖 Build the Houses. Locked positions become Houses, and each one's value is hidden. Land on a house again and it upgrades its material — Straw → Wood → Brick. New houses give 3 more respins; the sturdier the material, the richer the hidden prize (Brick holds the jackpots).

🐺 Huff & Puff. When the respins run out, the Big Bad Wolf blows every house down, revealing each hidden value one by one. You collect them all.

🌕 Free Games. Land 3 or more Full Moons to win Free Spins (3 = 6, 4 = 10, 5 = 15). During Free Games moneybags land far more often — the House feature triggers much more — and every win is doubled (×2). Land 3+ Moons again to retrigger.

RTP ~95.7%. All spins are resolved on the server — the reels only ever show a result already decided.
